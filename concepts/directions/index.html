Four directions

Each one changes the layout, the type and the rhythm, not only the color. The same catalog drives all of them, so the prices and service counts are identical throughout. Open one, then use the bar at the top of the page to jump between them.

  1. Lacquer BarLoud, young and confident. Color at full strength, type at full volume.The bold cosmetics houses: flat color blocks, condensed capitals, prices treated as graphics.View
  2. MaisonEvening, opulent and slow. The room lit like a campaign.The heritage beauty houses: full-bleed photography, dark ground, gilded hairlines, dramatic serif italics.View
  3. StudioQuiet and expensive. Very little on the page, and all of it deliberate.The minimalist skincare labels: enormous serif, tiny capitals, and a great deal of empty space.View
  4. PolishFresh and friendly. A color per collection, soft shapes, nothing intimidating.The new-wave beauty brands: pastel color blocking, rounded geometry, generous pill buttons.View
